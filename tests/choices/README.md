# Lyra's choices

Where IEEE 1800 leaves an answer to the tool -- an order it does not fix, a value it does not say, a
case its text does not reach, two clauses that read differently -- and Lyra has chosen one, a case
here holds Lyra to that choice so that it does not change by accident. A reading of two clauses that
disagree is ruled on in `docs/architecture/language_conformance.md`, and one Lyra takes against the
letter for a reason of its own is argued in a decision record; the case here holds what either says
Lyra does. A case has the shape of a conformance case (`../../conformance/README.md`), sits under
the clause whose silence it answers, and says in its prose what the standard leaves open and what
Lyra chose.

Nothing here is a statement of the standard, so only Lyra runs these cases; another tool answering
differently is not wrong. Where the standard bounds the answer to a set, the conformance case for
the same subject accepts every member of it, and the case here pins the one Lyra gives.
